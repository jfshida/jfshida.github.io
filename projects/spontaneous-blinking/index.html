Nature Nanotechnology · 2026 · Research project

Spontaneous blinking
in nanoparticle probes.

U-STORM uses spontaneously blinking upconverting nanoparticles for multicolor super-resolution imaging.

Contributing author

My contribution

My main contribution was helping with microscope design, operation, and optimization. I also provided input on data analysis.

Imaging approach

Blinking probes
for U-STORM

The team developed nanoparticles that switch between bright and dark states under continuous near-infrared illumination. Repeated localization of the same particle reduces uncertainty in its estimated position.

Study results

Multicolor imaging with one excitation source

The study demonstrated simultaneous blue and red imaging with a single excitation source, and applied the method to EGFR dimers and multimers on cell membranes.

Scientific details and measurements are described in the open-access paper.
