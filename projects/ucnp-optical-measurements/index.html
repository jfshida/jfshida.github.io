Study outcome

Measured performance.
Usable probes.

The resulting 10 nm probes combined distinct emission channels with sustained signal, enabling tracking for tens of minutes.

Brightness and channel separation depend on probe composition and excitation conditions; the measurements establish the conditions supporting the reported imaging performance.

Relevance to experimental platforms

Turning characterization into a design decision

Choosing an imaging probe requires quantitative evidence about its performance. My contribution demonstrates experimental characterization that connects a physical design choice with the quality of the resulting measurement.
