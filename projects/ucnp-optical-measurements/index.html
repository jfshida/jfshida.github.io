Optical characterization of
upconverting nanoparticles.

We measured the optical properties of 10 nm upconverting nanoparticles for long-term multicolor tracking.

Co-first author

My contribution

I carried out all optical measurements for this study, including single-particle brightness, emission spectra, photostability, and live-cell imaging.

Research question

Probe size and
optical performance

We compared particle size, brightness, photostability, and spectral separation to identify probes suitable for long-term multicolor tracking.

Brightness and channel separation depended on probe composition and excitation conditions. The paper reports these conditions alongside the optical measurements.
