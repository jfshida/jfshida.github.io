Research question

Receptor interactions
in living cells

We followed EGFR, HER2, and HER3 to measure dimer formation and dissociation, and examined how these interactions change with oncogenic mutations.

Methods

Microscope setup
and data analysis

01

Building the microscope

The custom instrument combined 976 nm excitation, switchable epi/TIRF illumination, four-channel detection, active focus stabilization, and a controlled live-cell environment.

  • Optical integration
  • Multichannel detection
  • Focus stability
02

Analyzing the data

The workflow used SLIMfast2 and Piscis/ByteTrack for trajectories, Bayesian hidden Markov models for diffusion states, GPU computation, and dimer-lifetime analysis in Python and MATLAB.

  • Python / MATLAB
  • Bayesian inference
  • GPU computation

Methods summarized from the paper’s STAR Methods.

Results

Main findings

We observed stable, ligand-independent dimers of mutant EGFR, as well as constitutive HER2 and HER3 homodimers.

Validation and limitations

Trajectory review

We reviewed trajectories manually and used simulations to assess the effects of track truncation. Crowded spots and out-of-focus motion could interrupt tracks. Sparse labeling meant that the measured dimer fractions were lower bounds.
